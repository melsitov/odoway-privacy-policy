1Introduction

VERST ("the App", "we", "us") is a vehicle expense tracker that helps you record what you spend on your cars and understand what they cost to run. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how it is stored, and who can reach it.

The short version is unusually short: the App is built to work entirely offline, and it does not have the technical means to send your information anywhere.

2Data stored on your device

Everything you enter is stored locally on your device, inside the App's private data directory, which other apps cannot read. Nothing is uploaded, automatically or otherwise.

  • Vehicles — name or registration plate, make, model, odometer readings.
  • Fuel fills — date, volume, cost, odometer reading.
  • Expenses — date, amount, category, notes, and any line items you add.
  • Categories — the built-in list and any you create yourself.
  • Settings — your chosen currency and unit system.

This data never leaves your device unless you deliberately export it (see section 5).

3No network access

VERST does not request the Android INTERNET permission. Android therefore prevents the App from opening any network connection at all. This is not a promise about how we behave — it is a restriction enforced by your operating system, and you can verify it yourself in the App's permission list.

There are no servers involved in running VERST, because there is nothing for the App to talk to. We never receive your data, so we cannot lose it, share it, or be compelled to hand it over.

4No third-party services

The App contains no advertising network, no analytics library, no crash reporting, and no marketing or attribution software of any kind. No third-party code in the App collects information about you.

What VERST does not do
Show advertisements or use an advertising identifier
Track your usage, sessions, or behaviour
Collect crash reports or diagnostic telemetry
Sell, rent, or share information with anyone
Create an account or profile for you

5Export, import and backup files

VERST can export your records to a CSV file for use in a spreadsheet, and can save a full backup file that you can restore later. You can also import a CSV you already have.

These are actions you take deliberately. When you export, your device's own file picker asks you where to save the file, and that choice is entirely yours. Once written, the file is an ordinary document on your device, governed by whatever you subsequently do with it.

We have no access to these files and do not receive a copy. If you move a backup to a cloud service or send it to someone, that service's privacy policy applies to it, not ours.

6Device permissions

VERST requests no Android runtime permissions — no camera, no location, no contacts, no storage, no notifications.

Task How it works without a permission
Choosing a file to import Uses the Android system file picker, which grants access to the single file you pick
Saving an export or backup Uses the Android system save dialog, which writes only where you choose

7Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep the App installed. You are in complete control of it:

  • Delete individual vehicles, entries or categories from inside the App.
  • Clear the App's storage from your device settings to remove everything at once.
  • Uninstall the App, which deletes its database entirely.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf and no deletion request for you to make.

8Children's privacy

VERST is intended for adults who own or run a vehicle. It is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

9Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. If a future version of VERST introduces features that handle data differently — for example optional accounts or cloud backup — this policy will be updated before that version is released.
